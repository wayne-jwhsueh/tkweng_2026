<h1>About Teng-Ko Weng</h1>

<section class="about-intro hero-card">
  <div>
    <img class="about-portrait" src="/images/about/weng_0001__473x600.webp" alt="Portrait of Teng-Ko Weng" loading="lazy" />
  </div>
  <div class="about-copy">
    <p>
      Teng-Ko Weng was born in 1960 in Taipei, Taiwan, into a family of modest means; paintbrushes were a luxury
      he could not afford. In third grade, he entered a school-wide painting competition for the first time and
      won first place. His older brother, seeing his talent, gave him a box of colorful crayons as encouragement.
      Treasuring the gift, he went on to win first place three times in all, and from that moment he set his heart
      on becoming an artist.
    </p>
    <p>
      In his younger years, he survived a serious bout of meningitis. After recovering, he unexpectedly discovered
      that he could paint with both hands and both feet at the same time, and he was invited to demonstrate this
      on two major Taiwanese television programs. He regards it not as a performance, but as a living testimony
      of God&rsquo;s healing grace.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

  <div class="about-copy">
    <figure class="about-award-figure">
      <img src="/images/about/award_SFCA_2016.jpg" alt="Federation of Canadian Artists SFCA recognition" loading="lazy" />
      <figcaption class="meta">Federation of Canadian Artists (SFCA) Senior Signature Member</figcaption>

    <p>
      After graduating from art school, he worked as a sculptor, a traditional Taiwanese folk artist, a painting
      instructor at Shih Chien University&rsquo;s continuing education center, and a painting teacher at the Taipei
      YMCA and YWCA, and he was a represented artist with galleries in Taipei. His works earned multiple
      first-place awards. He has published a personal art collection, a gospel painting calendar, and his
      autobiography. His paintings have been exhibited in museums and galleries across Taiwan and Canada. One of
      his sculptures is held in the permanent collection of Taipei Veterans General Hospital, and one of his
      paintings is held in the permanent collection of a public educational institution in Langley, British
      Columbia.
    </p>
    <p>
      In 1995, he immigrated to British Columbia, Canada, through the Self-Employed Artist immigration program, a
      category for artists with distinguished professional credentials. He has lived and worked in British
      Columbia ever since.
    </p>
    <p>
      In Canada, he has continued to paint, exhibit internationally, and teach. In 2016, he was awarded Senior
      Signature Member status (SFCA) by the Federation of Canadian Artists, the organization&rsquo;s highest
      membership level, and he has served as a juror for FCA exhibitions. In 2026, his painting
      &ldquo;Light of Hope&rdquo; received an Honourable Mention at the FCA Medals Exhibition, and his work was
      also selected for the FCA&rsquo;s On The Edge exhibition.
    </p>
    <p>
      In 2020, he founded <a class="about-link" href="https://www.gloriousart.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Glorious Art Institute</a>, bringing over five decades of creative and teaching
      experience to the next generation of artists and university applicants.
    </p>
    <p>
      In recent years, he founded Trinitas Pictura, an art movement rooted in Trinitarian faith, exploring how
      uniqueness, order, and unity can coexist in the act of creation. Through decades of painting and teaching, he
      has continually discovered the Creator&rsquo;s imprint in the elements of art, and he has shaped that
      discovery into a distinctive visual language and teaching philosophy.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>
