Not long ago, I was invited by the program *Small People, Big Stories* (《小人物大故事》) to share the story of my life's journey. The theme of this episode is *Through the Valley, Grace Follows*. It speaks of the low points and struggles in my life, and bears witness to how God upheld and guided me and my family through every hardship.

I am only an ordinary person, one of the "small people." If there is anything worth sharing, it is all by God's grace. If you have time, I warmly invite you to watch, and I hope this testimony brings you a little encouragement and comfort.
